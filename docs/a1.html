2. How many LEDs could you blink independently with your arduino? How much current would that draw?

There are 14 digital pins and 6 analog pins that can be used to blink up to 20 LEDs independently. Individually, each pin can supply about 20 mA to light up an LED, however, the Arduino has a limit of 200mA. This means that we cannot blink all of the LEDs at maximum brightness, but we can calculate how much current each LED should get. Given we have a limit of 200mA and 20 pins, that means each LED should be getting 10mA of current. Assuming that we use red LEDs that use 2V, then we can figure that (5-2)/0.010 = 300 ohms of resistance per pin.
